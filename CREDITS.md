# Credits and asset provenance

## Curriculum and interface

This release preserves the curriculum and interface from Sharan's existing **Open AI Engineering Curriculum** project. The public source has been separated from its original hosting scaffold. The standalone build uses React and Vite; it does not contain the original hosting configuration, account integration, or repository history.

The course ordering, selection notes, specialization questions, and project suggestions are editorial material in that project. They are not statements of endorsement by the linked speakers or the AI Engineer channel.

## Video resources

[AI Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/@aiDotEngineer) publishes the linked talks and workshops. Speaker names and the source link remain visible for each resource. Resource titles may be shortened for display. Current YouTube titles and channel URLs are recorded in `docs/resource-link-audit.json`.

This repository contains links, titles, and short selection notes. It does not redistribute the videos, audio, thumbnails, or transcripts. Rights in the linked media belong to their respective owners.

All three are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Notices were recovered from the corresponding `ofl` directories of the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), and accompany the fonts here. They apply to the font files, not to the whole application.

## Project artwork

`public/og.png` and `public/favicon.svg` are retained from the owner's original project source. The preview graphic contains the project's title and geometric artwork; it does not reproduce a video thumbnail or a speaker image.
